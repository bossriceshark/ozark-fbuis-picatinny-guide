Ozark Armament Front Sight Adjustment Tool

Zeroing an AR-15 style front sight post is awkward without the right wrench. The Ozark Armament front sight adjustment tool is a compact metal tool made for A1 and A2 posts with four or five prongs, so you can raise or lower elevation without using a cartridge rim or an improvised bit that can chew the post.

What it fits

Use it on standard A1 (five-prong) and A2 (four-prong) front sight posts found on many AR-15 and sporting rifles, including fixed front sight bases and many flip-up front sights that use the same post style. Place the matching end over the post and turn to change elevation one click at a time.

Why a dedicated tool helps

Range bags already hold magazines, ear pro, and a rear-sight tool. Adding a purpose-built front sight tool keeps adjustments consistent and reduces slips that scratch the post or your optic mount. The milled center gives a better grip with wet hands or gloves than a smooth rod.

How to use it at the range

Secure the rifle on bags or a rest. Fire a group, then adjust elevation with the tool and confirm windage at the rear sight. Re-shoot and repeat until point of aim matches point of impact at your chosen distance. Keep the tool in the same pouch as your rear sight wrench so both irons stay serviceable.
