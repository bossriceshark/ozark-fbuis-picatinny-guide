Generic safe practice

  1. Seat the recoil lug fully in a Picatinny slot before tightening.
  2. Snug evenly until the sight does not slide under hand pressure—stop before rounding the hex.
  3. If you use threadlocker, prefer a removable low-strength formula on clean threads; avoid permanent lockers on small sight screws unless the maker says so.
  4. Re-check screws after the first range session.
  5. Never substitute “as tight as possible” for seating the lug—crushing a rail or screw helps no one.
