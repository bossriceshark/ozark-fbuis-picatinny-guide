Setting up a folding front and rear BUIS pair on an AR-15

A matched flip-up pair gives a flat-top AR-15 a mechanical sight line that stays folded until the optic fails. Ozark Armament sells one such set: front and rear sights machined from aluminum, spring loaded, and made to clamp onto any Picatinny rail. See the AR-15 flip up iron sights listing for the full details.

Mounting the pair

Place the rear sight near the receiver end of the top rail and the front sight as far forward as your handguard allows. A longer sight radius makes small aiming errors easier to see. The set ships with a hex screwdriver, so you can tighten both clamps at the bench without extra tools.

Zero and apertures

Both sights adjust for windage and elevation with milled dials. The rear lets you toggle between a precision aperture for slow, careful shots and a larger CQB aperture for close, fast work. Zero the irons alone first, then check where they sit in your red dot's window when raised.

Folded until needed

Folded down, these folding iron sights stay low and out of the optic's view. Flip them up and they can co-witness with the dot or serve on their own.
