BUIS on pistol-caliber carbines

PCCs often wear red-dots on Picatinny receivers just like rifle carbines. Soft recoil is easy on clamps, but blowback vibration can still walk loose screws. A matched flip-up set such as FBUIS-B works on PCC rails that are true Picatinny with room for front and rear.

PCC-specific notes

  1. Seat lugs in slots; snug hex screws after the first magazine of live fire.
  2. Zero at PCC distances you shoot (often 15–50 yards).
  3. Watch fold clearance around cheek risers and enclosure-style braces.
