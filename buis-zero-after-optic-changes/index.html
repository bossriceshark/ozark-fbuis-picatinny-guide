Checking BUIS zero after optic changes

Changing optic height, ring position, or handguard torque can alter cheek weld and how you see co-witness—even if BUIS clamps never moved. After any optic work, confirm backup zero with live groups, not assumptions.

View FBUIS-B on Amazon

When to re-confirm

  • New optic or different mount height
  • Optic removed and reinstalled
  • Handguard removed (front sight rail may shift)
  • Witness marks on windage/elevation dials no longer match

Quick confirm

  1. Flip BUIS up; same aperture as your recorded zero.
  2. Fire a small group at your BUIS zero distance.
  3. Adjust dials only after clamps pass a wiggle test.
