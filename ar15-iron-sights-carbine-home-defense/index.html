Practical setup for short-range carbine work

  1. Run your primary optic if you have one; keep FBUIS-B folded until needed.
  2. Zero irons at a realistic defensive/carbine distance you actually shoot (often 25–50 yards)—do not invent a 300-yard iron zero you never practice.
  3. Train the large CQB rear aperture for fast acquisition; know how to flip to the smaller aperture for tighter groups on a square range.
  4. After any optic or handguard change, reconfirm clamp torque and a confirmation group.
