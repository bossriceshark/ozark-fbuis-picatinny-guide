Carry Handle Scope Mount

Some AR-pattern rifles keep the factory carry handle. That handle sits high and leaves no flat Picatinny rail on top for a modern optic. A detachable Picatinny carry handle scope mount solves that by clamping to the handle and giving you a short rail where a red dot or low-profile optic can sit.

Shoppers look for this part under names like carry handle mount, carry handle scope mount, or Picatinny universal detachable mount. The job is the same: keep the carry handle in place and still run an optic above it.

What to check before you buy

  • Confirm your rifle still has the solid A2-style carry handle, not a flat-top upper with a separate rear sight.
  • Look for a mount that clamps securely and presents a Picatinny section long enough for your optic footprint.
  • Check the Amazon listing photos for how the mount sits on the handle and whether the optic clears the rear aperture.
