Aluminum flip-up BUIS on a Picatinny rail

Polymer backup sights save a few ounces. Aluminum ones trade that for rigidity under clamp torque and recoil. Ozark Armament’s FBUIS-B is the metal route: a matched front-and-rear flip-up set cut from aircraft-grade aluminum, spring-loaded, and clamped to standard Picatinny.

View FBUIS-B on Amazon

What changes with metal

  • Clamp faces that don’t crush as easily when you seat the recoil lug.
  • Windage and elevation dials that stay crisp after zeroing.
  • A rear sight that toggles between a precision aperture and a faster CQB aperture.
  • Fold-flat profile so the set co-witnesses with many red-dots when up, and stays out of the window when folded.

Mount checklist

  1. Pick rail slots so front and rear heights match your optic plan.
  2. Drop the lug into a slot; tighten with the included hex driver—firm, not stripped.
  3. Flip up, confirm co-witness, then zero.
  4. Work the detent a few times; a drop of light oil on the pivot helps if travel feels stiff out of the box.

Pairing flip-up backup sights with a red dot sight

A red dot sight is fast for both-eyes-open shooting, but it runs on a battery and can be damaged. Flip-up backup iron sights stay folded under the optic and come up if the dot goes out. If you are choosing an optic to pair with the FBUIS-B set, the Ozark Armament Rhino red dot sight is one option to look at. Check the optic's height and your rail space before buying, and confirm co-witness at the range.
