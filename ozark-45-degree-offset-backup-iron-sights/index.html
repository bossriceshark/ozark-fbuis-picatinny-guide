Ozark Armament 45° Offset Backup Iron Sights

If your AR-15 wears an LPVO or a magnified scope, traditional inline flip-up sights often get in the way. Canted irons solve that by sitting on the rail at a 45 degree angle, so you can roll the rifle slightly and pick up a sight picture without removing or adjusting the optic. That makes them a fast fallback when a battery dies, a lens fogs, or a target suddenly appears too close for magnification.

Ozark Armament makes a matched front and rear set of 45 degree offset iron sights built for Picatinny rails. The pair is machined aluminum, low profile, and clamps securely to standard rail sections, keeping weight and snag points to a minimum.

Setup tips

  • Mount both sights on the same side, usually the side of your support hand roll.
  • Keep the sight radius as long as your rail allows for better precision.
  • Zero at 50 yards, then confirm at 100 to check point of impact.
  • Practice the roll-to-irons transition during dry fire so it becomes automatic.
